How quickly are services deployed?

Supported services are provisioned automatically after checkout and payment confirmation. The customer portal shows the current provisioning status.

FoxNetwork BV is a Belgian company based in Lembeek, Halle. Hosting products use European infrastructure.

Is Discord bot hosting really free?

Yes. The current starter product is €0.00 and does not require a payment card. Resource limits apply, and paid options can be added as your bot grows.

Can I choose my VPS operating system?

Yes. Available operating-system images are shown during VPS checkout, before the service is created.

Do game servers include a control panel?

Yes. Game hosting is managed through a customer control panel with service controls and the options configured for the selected product.
