Questions

The important details.

Is FoxFlix available now?

Yes. Open FoxFlix in a modern browser, create your account and choose the All-In period that suits you.

What can I watch?

The current catalogue is shown inside FoxFlix. Library metadata and playback remain private until you sign in.

Where can I watch?

FoxFlix is browser-first and responsive, so you can use it on desktop, tablet and mobile screens with a supported modern browser.

How does payment work?

You select a fixed access period and continue to Mollie's secure checkout. FoxFlix activates access after confirmed payment.
