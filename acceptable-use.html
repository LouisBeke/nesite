1. Purpose and Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) defines the acceptable and prohibited use of services provided by FoxNetwork.

This policy applies to all customers, users, administrators, authorised users, sub-users, and third parties who access or use services provided by FoxNetwork.

Services covered by this AUP include, but are not limited to:

  • Web hosting
  • Game server hosting
  • Minecraft server hosting
  • VPS and cloud hosting
  • Discord bot hosting
  • Application hosting
  • Database hosting
  • Storage and related infrastructure
  • Network and server services
  • Any other hosting or digital infrastructure service provided by FoxNetwork

By purchasing, accessing, or using a FoxNetwork service, the customer agrees to comply with this AUP.

2. General Acceptable Use

FoxNetwork services may be used for legitimate personal, commercial, development, gaming, community, and other lawful purposes.

Customers must use our infrastructure responsibly and in a manner that does not negatively affect FoxNetwork, our infrastructure, other customers, third parties, or the general public.

Customers are responsible for maintaining the security of their accounts, applications, servers, passwords, API credentials, and other authentication information.

3. Compliance with Laws and Regulations

Customers must comply with all applicable local, Belgian, European Union, and international laws and regulations.

FoxNetwork services may not be used to facilitate, promote, conceal, or conduct illegal activities.

Customers must also comply with applicable trade restrictions, export controls, and financial or economic sanctions where relevant.

A customer may not knowingly make FoxNetwork services available to a person or organisation where doing so would violate applicable sanctions or other legal restrictions.

4. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are prohibited on FoxNetwork services:

Illegal Content and Activities

Customers may not use our services to host, distribute, facilitate, or promote content or activities that violate applicable law. This includes, where applicable, fraud, scams, theft, unlawful trafficking, illegal marketplaces, or services designed primarily to facilitate criminal activity.

Malware and Malicious Software

Customers may not knowingly create, host, execute, distribute, or control:

  • Malware
  • Ransomware
  • Viruses
  • Trojans
  • Botnets
  • Credential-stealing software
  • Malicious scripts
  • Cryptojacking software
  • Other software intended to compromise or damage systems without authorisation

Legitimate security research or testing may only be performed on systems for which the customer has explicit authorisation.

Network Attacks

The following activities are prohibited:

  • Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks
  • Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks
  • DDoS-for-hire or “booter/stresser” services
  • Packet flooding
  • Unauthorised port scanning
  • Network disruption
  • Exploitation of third-party systems without authorisation
  • Attempts to bypass or compromise security systems

Unauthorised Access

Customers may not attempt to gain unauthorised access to accounts, computers, networks, servers, applications, databases, or other systems. This includes credential stuffing, password cracking, phishing, exploitation, or similar activities performed without explicit permission.

Spam and Messaging Abuse

FoxNetwork services may not be used to send unsolicited bulk communications or operate infrastructure primarily intended for spam. Prohibited activities include:

  • Email spam
  • SMS spam
  • Messaging spam
  • Phishing
  • Fraudulent communications
  • Email address harvesting
  • Deliberate circumvention of anti-spam systems

Legitimate transactional, administrative, or opt-in marketing communications are permitted provided they comply with applicable law.

Fraud and Impersonation

Customers may not use FoxNetwork services for fraudulent activities, including:

  • Payment fraud
  • Identity theft
  • Phishing
  • Deliberate impersonation for fraudulent purposes
  • Fake payment or banking websites
  • Services intended to obtain credentials or financial information deceptively

Proxy, VPN and Tunnelling Services

Private proxy, VPN, tunnelling, reverse-proxy, or similar technologies may only be used for legitimate purposes.

Customers may not operate these technologies to:

  • Conceal illegal activity
  • Facilitate cyberattacks
  • Circumvent sanctions or legal restrictions
  • Operate abusive public proxy networks
  • Facilitate spam, fraud, credential attacks, or other prohibited activity

FoxNetwork may restrict or prohibit high-risk proxy or tunnelling services where they create an unacceptable security, abuse, or compliance risk.

Intellectual Property

Customers must respect applicable copyright, trademark, and other intellectual-property rights.

FoxNetwork services may not knowingly be used for the unauthorised distribution of copyrighted material where such distribution violates applicable law.

Valid intellectual-property complaints may be investigated and acted upon.

Harmful or Abusive Content

FoxNetwork services may not be used for content or activities that are unlawful or that create a serious and credible risk of harm to others.

FoxNetwork may take action against services used for threats, targeted harassment, exploitation, or other harmful conduct where required by law or where necessary to protect our infrastructure, customers, or third parties.

5. Resource and Infrastructure Abuse

Customers must use allocated resources reasonably and according to the specifications of their purchased service.

Customers may not intentionally interfere with the performance or availability of FoxNetwork infrastructure or other customers.

Activities causing excessive or abnormal CPU, memory, storage, bandwidth, network, or I/O usage may be restricted where they threaten the stability or security of our infrastructure.

Where reasonably possible, FoxNetwork will contact the customer before taking action for ordinary resource-related issues.

Immediate action may be taken when necessary to protect infrastructure or other users.

6. Security Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for appropriately securing their services.

This includes, where applicable:

  • Using strong passwords
  • Protecting authentication credentials
  • Installing security updates
  • Keeping software reasonably up to date
  • Correctly configuring applications and services
  • Restricting unnecessary network access
  • Responding to security incidents

A compromised server or account remains subject to this AUP even when abusive activity was caused by an unauthorised third party.

FoxNetwork may temporarily restrict or suspend a compromised service to prevent further harm.

7. Customers, Third Parties and Sub-Users

Customers are responsible for activity performed through their FoxNetwork services, including activity by:

  • Employees
  • Administrators
  • Players
  • Community members
  • Customers of the customer
  • Contractors
  • Sub-users
  • Other persons granted access to the service

Customers providing access to third parties must take reasonable measures to ensure those users comply with this AUP.

Allowing another person to access a service does not remove the account holder's responsibilities under this policy.

8. Reselling and Third-Party Services

Where FoxNetwork permits the resale or redistribution of services, the primary customer remains responsible for ensuring that downstream users comply with this AUP.

FoxNetwork reserves the right to take action against the underlying service when a downstream user violates this policy.

9. Monitoring and Investigation

FoxNetwork respects the privacy of its customers and does not require customers to submit their private content for routine inspection.

However, FoxNetwork reserves the right to investigate suspected abuse, security incidents, fraud, legal violations, or violations of this AUP.

Investigations may be initiated following:

  • Automated security alerts
  • Abuse reports
  • Network monitoring
  • Fraud detection
  • Complaints
  • Reports from infrastructure providers
  • Requests from competent authorities
  • Other credible evidence of misuse

FoxNetwork may review information reasonably necessary to investigate and resolve an incident, subject to applicable law.

10. Enforcement

If FoxNetwork reasonably determines that a customer has violated this AUP, FoxNetwork may take appropriate action depending on the severity and urgency of the violation.

Actions may include:

  • Issuing a warning
  • Requesting corrective action
  • Restricting network access
  • Blocking specific ports or traffic
  • Removing or disabling prohibited content
  • Temporarily suspending a service
  • Permanently terminating a service
  • Terminating the customer's account
  • Refusing future services

Where reasonably possible and appropriate, customers will be given an opportunity to correct minor violations.

FoxNetwork reserves the right to immediately suspend or terminate services without prior notice where necessary to prevent serious abuse, fraud, security incidents, illegal activity, significant harm to third parties, or damage to FoxNetwork infrastructure.

11. Abuse Reports and Cooperation

Customers must reasonably cooperate with FoxNetwork when investigating legitimate abuse or security complaints relating to their services.

Failure to respond to serious or repeated abuse reports may result in restrictions or suspension.

Knowingly submitting false or malicious abuse reports is itself considered misuse.

12. Legal Requests

FoxNetwork may cooperate with competent law-enforcement agencies, courts, regulatory authorities, or other legally authorised entities where required by applicable law.

Information will only be disclosed where FoxNetwork has a lawful basis or legal obligation to do so.

13. Emergency Measures

FoxNetwork may take immediate technical measures where a service creates an urgent threat to:

  • Network stability
  • Infrastructure security
  • Other customers
  • Third-party systems
  • The general public

Such measures may include temporary isolation, traffic filtering, network restrictions, or suspension.

14. Repeated Violations

Repeated violations of this AUP may result in permanent termination of the affected service or customer account.

Attempts to circumvent a previous suspension, restriction, or termination may also result in termination.

15. No Guarantee of Uninterrupted Service

Compliance with this AUP does not guarantee uninterrupted availability of a service.

Maintenance, technical failures, security incidents, upstream-provider issues, force majeure events, or other circumstances may affect service availability.

Any applicable service guarantees are governed separately by FoxNetwork's Terms and Conditions or applicable service agreement.

16. Changes to this Policy

FoxNetwork may update this Acceptable Use Policy when necessary to reflect changes to our services, infrastructure, security requirements, legal obligations, or abuse-prevention practices.

The current version will be published on the FoxNetwork website.

Material changes may be communicated to customers where appropriate or legally required.

Continued use of FoxNetwork services following an applicable update constitutes acceptance of the updated policy to the extent permitted by law.

17. Contact and Abuse Reports

Questions regarding this Acceptable Use Policy or reports of suspected abuse should be submitted through the official FoxNetwork contact or support channels published on our website.

When reporting abuse, please provide sufficient information for us to identify and investigate the affected service.
